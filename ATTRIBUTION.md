# Attribution and reuse

Marksheet is distributed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

The license permits commercial and noncommercial use, copying, modification,
merging, publication, distribution, sublicensing, and sale. Its condition is
that the copyright and permission notice remain in copies or substantial
portions of the project.

This means forks and substantial derivative works retain a reference to the
Marksheet project through this notice:

```text
Copyright (c) 2026 Marksheet project contributors
```

No royalty, fee, source-disclosure requirement, reciprocal-license condition,
or other commercial obligation is imposed.

When practical, derivative projects are also invited—but not required beyond
the license text—to say:

```text
Based on the Marksheet project.
```

Keeping that sentence optional preserves use of an unmodified, widely
understood permissive license.
